# Basic Haskell grammar

Many grammatical constructs in Haskell are just different ways of writing the same underlying logic. The grammatican constructs we present in this section do not make Haskell more _expressive_, but they do make Haskell more _ergonomic_ to read and write.

These topics are also covered by a number of other Haskell resources. You may want to look at a few different resources and see which explanation style resonates most with you.
