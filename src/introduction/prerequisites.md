# Prerequisite knowledge

The rest of this introduction is dedicated to getting new user up to speed quickly. Since a new user may entail new to Haskell and/or new to hardware, we provide sections on both. You don't have to be an expert to start in Clash - I knew neither when I started. You just have to listen, answer questions as they come up with one of the many online resources, and have a little (or a medium amount of) patience.

If you are already familiar with one or the other, feel free to skim the sections. You can always refer back to them later.

Or if you are tired of all the talk and want to start writing Clash already, feel free to jump to [Chapter 1: Writing basic circuits](../chapter_01/intro.md).
